What if… you needed one button?#

In Happy, we're trying one door instead:

All new work starts with a stem cell.

Create makes a row called New. Just say what you want done: it names itself, grows its own icon, and the sidebar self-organizes, slotting it into the right section, even if that section is Distractions.

What happens next?#

Once you've said enough, the stem cell morphs into the right thing: it finds the repository to work in and loops in the people who should know.

Naming, icons and sections work today. Repositories and people are next on our roadmap.

Every boomerang comes back#

With today's models, starting work is cheap, and a little addictive. You throw one boomerang after another: a quick fix, an experiment, a side quest. They fly far, and it's fun. Then they all start coming back at once, and you're overwhelmed.

That's why Happy has a dedicated focus agent whose only job is to keep you on point. It puts new work next to related work, parks side quests in Distractions, and keeps the sidebar in shape, so when a boomerang comes back, you know where to catch it.
