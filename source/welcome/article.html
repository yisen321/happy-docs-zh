Welcome#

Happy is an open-source harness for orchestrating coding agents: Claude, Codex, Grok, Gemini, and more in one desktop app, reusing the subscriptions you already pay for. Agents hand work to subagents on other models, run under one permission model and sandbox, and keep going through restarts. The phone app, remote servers, and teams are parts of it.

Download it for macOS, Windows, or Linux and open it. Happy starts its own agent runtime and picks up the Claude, Codex, and Grok sign-ins on the machine.

What you get#

  • Every model, native. Claude gets Claude Code's prompts and tools, GPT models get Codex's, Grok gets Grok Build's. Gemini runs in Google's Antigravity, and any Agent Client Protocol (ACP) agent can run too (beta). Switch models and the transcript stays.
  • Orchestration. Subagents on any model, agents that message each other, scheduled messages, and persistent bots.
  • Your subscriptions. No Happy account for model access. Credentials come from the Claude Code, Codex, and Grok CLI sign-ins already on the machine.
  • Chief of Staff. The first bot in the app. It sets Happy up in conversation: adds model accounts, imports projects, pairs your phone, deploys a remote agent, creates a team, upgrades Happy.
  • Durable sessions. Every conversation and subagent survives a closed window and a restart, and takes the next message with full context.
  • Auto mode. Reads and workspace edits run freely. Everything else is sandboxed by the OS. The few actions that cross the boundary are reviewed automatically, never by prompting you.
  • Workspaces. Parallel work gets its own Git worktree on its own branch.
  • Phone. The same sessions in the Happy app for iPhone and Android, end-to-end encrypted. The relay carries ciphertext only.
  • Multiplayer. Deploy a team server and work together. Everyone signs in as themselves and shares the same projects and sessions.
  • Skills, MCP. Markdown files and TOML entries. An agent can write both.
  • MIT. Read it, fork it, ship your own build.

What does Happy cost?#

Happy is free and MIT licensed, and the models run on your own Claude, Codex, and Grok subscriptions. Voice in the phone app is the only thing we charge for: 20 free minutes per 30 days, then up to 5 hours per 30 days on the in-app subscription. With your own ElevenLabs agent there is no limit from Happy. See Voice.
